I’m now in Croatia, staying with some friends in Zagreb. I met Ivana in 2006 when we both were in Siena in Italy studying Italian. Her husband Nikola is a tour guide and their son Ino is 4 yo and delightful. They live in an apartment up the mountains from the city. Here’s Ino...

![]({{ '/tex/assets/uploads/2019/03/A0D1CF0F-68B3-416D-B884-872D30E47F12.jpeg' | relative_url }})

Here are the rest of us...

![]({{ '/tex/assets/uploads/2019/03/A426D68B-8EA2-45F9-B7F6-2061139BD2BF.jpeg' | relative_url }})

They have a place on the island of Lošinj, which is in the Adriatic Sea. They live there during the tourist season, and from there manage several other properties which tourists live in. They picked me up from Zagreb’s modern and little airport on Friday, and we drove to the island, over some bridges and a ferry, arriving in mid afternoon. The weather was sunny and splendid, and the view from their place, within spitting distance of the beach, was stunning.

![]({{ '/tex/assets/uploads/2019/03/A995E396-9279-4C3A-9A88-0D9E3321C01A.jpeg' | relative_url }})

On Saturday they dropped me in the town which is clustered around a harbour and tidily organised.

![]({{ '/tex/assets/uploads/2019/03/D836CCE2-48CA-4B2E-A39C-BD0120846425.jpeg' | relative_url }})

I visited the main tourist attraction of the place, the Apoxyomenos museum. This has only one thing in it, a bronze from the first century BC of a young athlete. It was discovered in the sea nearby about 20 years ago. It is in superb condition and the museum charts its restoration in interesting detail. The final coup de grâce is the statue itself in a totally white room. It is quite amazing. Sadly the invigilator there enforced the rule of no photos, but there were several portholes through which you could get the photo from outside the room. So I took a few,

![]({{ '/tex/assets/uploads/2019/03/C8496ABB-A8EE-4443-8C75-4049B89037B2.jpeg' | relative_url }})

but realised on looking at them that they have something of a voyeristic flavour...perving down a ventilation shaft into a white room where a starkers bloke with a bronzish suntan is not doing much!

Here’s an article about the statue... <https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatian_Apoxyomenos>

We returned to Zagreb in the afternoon, along excellent roads passing through mountains covered with wintering deciduous trees, some with the first tinges of green.

Nikola headed off to India to manage a tour group for 10 days, so on Monday, Ivana, who is also a tour guide, drove me to a castle tourist attraction (that’s it in the background and the attractive tourists in the foreground)...

![]({{ '/tex/assets/uploads/2019/03/8EF66D4B-3DBB-4445-803A-A675B42490ED.jpeg' | relative_url }})

and in the afternoon as the temperature outside plummeted to 4 degrees and snow fell on the surrounding mountains, we went to a trad Croatian restaurant and had a magnificent meal of grilled squid. It was so delicious.

![]({{ '/tex/assets/uploads/2019/03/C42945A3-A76F-469A-A223-FF15858F16E0.jpeg' | relative_url }})

On Tuesday we did a tour of the city sights including the cathedral, which looks medieval, but is in fact gothic revival, ( same architectural innovation that produced St Paul’s in Melbourne, where medieval styles were revived in the 19th century)

![]({{ '/tex/assets/uploads/2019/03/525CEB7D-85D7-411F-AE03-525B72D77F0A.jpeg' | relative_url }})

There was a church here from the 12 th century, and this is the latest version after pillage by the Tartars and earthquakes more recently.

![]({{ '/tex/assets/uploads/2019/03/C4074157-8C86-4DBE-AD89-872FCB6DD3BE.jpeg' | relative_url }})

We also went to a place called ‘the museum of broken relationships’. It’s an idea, started here but which has spread, where people donate an object which is significant for a broken relationship, and write a small passage to tell the story. It was fascinating. Here are some examples...

![]({{ '/tex/assets/uploads/2019/03/6838A8F9-984D-47FD-BD23-EE7D450BC81D.jpeg' | relative_url }})

And

![]({{ '/tex/assets/uploads/2019/03/69FA914D-E4DF-43C7-A8B4-A9BF60696079.jpeg' | relative_url }})

The stories were splendidly written and each exhibit direct and non sentimental.

Later we had a magnificent lunch at a 5star bistro. My main course was some swordfish chunks, which were relatively small, so you can probably intuit that they were pretty pricey, but delish. The frying pan lookalike is only about 15cm by 15 cm, so is quite small...but delicious food doesn’t need to be in massive quantities.

![]({{ '/tex/assets/uploads/2019/03/81B4D764-23E7-40F5-91C3-56D5CB7556C8.jpeg' | relative_url }})

It seems that Croatian mothers follow the rule that if you are a bloke then you have to eat, so I’ve been inundated with food, and treated like the sultan. I suspect that the jeans that I bought in Tunis, relatively close fitting before I arrived here in Zagreb, may be fitting even closer by tomorrow.

And tomorrow, Wednesday I fly to Paris, wait an hour then reach Madrid by 2.15 pm. Olay!

Enjoy the moment

T
