Hello everyone,

It feels really good to be back on the road again; catching up with old sights and friends and making and seeing some new ones.

I got to Kuala Lumpur on the 16th, and discovered even cheaper ways to get from the airport to the city. In the past I have taken (or more correctly it has taken me) the KLIA Express, a high speed train, which takes 30 min to get to the central station, and costs around $20. It’s fast, has free wifi and is comfortable. Then there is the Grabcar, a sort of Uber, which takes a little longer, is a bit more expensive, but one is in a car. Then there is the skybus...about $5, is comfortable, but takes an hour. I tend to think of savings in terms of massage hours...so I saved enough for almost an hour of massage by going on the bus! Well worth it.

The airbnb that I’m in is relatively central, is on the 14th floor of a huge apartment block, and has this for a view over the city...

The bedroom is a bit cellular, the attached bathroom is fine and the living space airy and light. Grace, the woman whose place it is is very hospitable and friendly. All this for $18 a night.

The weather is hottish, very humid, and around 4 every afternoon there is a torrential downpour. Nothing much changes it seems.
